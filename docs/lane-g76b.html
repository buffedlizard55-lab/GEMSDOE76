Answering this session's question: why 0.2778 leads, and can we beat it?

Why the incumbent leads (measured, see why-02778.md). h33-h33-2-b2-…-zeros (0.2778 USER-REPORTED) is not a new model: byte-audit shows it is the supervised H19-5 multi-physics surface thinned to 44,090 isolated dots (D2.8, 0.2600), then pruned of the 6,436 dots within 200 m of the USGS/INGENIOUS catalogue → 37,654 dots. Three metric facts do the work: β/α = 4 makes recall cheap (a marginal dot pays whenever its kernel credit exceeds 0.056 at s = 0.2778); the 300 m triangular max-kernel makes well-spaced isolated dots nearly as good as lines at ~⅓ the false-positive cost; and catalogue pixels are masked in scoring, so near-catalogue dots are pure FP against expert-mapped new-fault truth — deleting them was free. The -zeros encoding (0.0 instead of NaN outside the footprint) only guarantees passing the portal's whole-raster [0,1] range check; the in-footprint bytes of the -nan/-zeros twins are identical.

Can we beat it? Not with this lane, and not with any emission tweak: the whole B2 prune was worth ~+0.007 live, while the leader (0.4413) implies roughly twice the truth coverage of the incumbent — a surface-quality gap. This session tested the one ingredient the family had not tried at feature level — 2-D patch context (directional persistence, local support density, cross-scale magnetic edge agreement, strain-belt gating) — inside a fold-honest supervised head. Result: negative on every instrument (below random on A and C76-pied). The supervised head concentrated its mass in a few hot regions (top-5 20 km blocks hold 0.286 of all dots; max 2,249 dots/block vs H33's 638; 104 blocks occupied vs H33's 158), and per-quadrant transfer was unstable (fold-honest fitted-prob AUCs 0.658, 0.508, 0.654, 0.665). Concentrated-but-misplaced mass pays full FP under a recall-weighted max-cover metric.

What remains credible (unchanged from why-02778.md §5): a full-scale 2-D spatial-context model (patch CNN / U--Net, the organizer's reference architecture — never run at full scale by any of the ~80 family lanes, compute-bound), better selector calibration, or genuinely new data. Per-pixel and hand-crafted-context routes are now both measured negatives in this repo.
