Release sequence (once a candidate passes all gates)

  1. Establish inputs and lane; place hash-pinned shared feature caches; authenticate sample/catalogue and data eligibility; refresh the scoped raster registry.
  2. Validate science: feature-alone leakage canaries (AUC ≤ 0.90), buffered whole-segment spatial hide-and-recover, pooled DTI with version, withheld positives and 95% CI; uniqueness vs every prior.
  3. Package once via the shared writer (single-band float32 GeoTIFF, optional one-TIFF ZIP); no silent clipping/repair; values already in [0,1]; exterior zeroed; catalogue pixels masked exactly.
  4. Separate selection from generation: holdout improvement vs current best, selector approval, current weekly cap on the organizer submission page.
  5. Sign in to the official competition, choose the approved .tif or one-TIFF .zip, copy the unique submission name and a note of at most 140 characters from the receipt, submit, and save the organizer receipt. Only then label a score ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED.

Only after a genuinely approved file is published

  1. Download the file explicitly marked as the approved prediction (not a reference or diagnostic). Compare its SHA-256 against its adjacent receipt.
  2. Sign in to the official competition, review eligibility/rules, open its New submission form.
  3. Choose the approved single-band .tif or its one-TIFF .zip as “File to submit”. Do not upload a document.
  4. Copy the artifact's unique submission name and its note of at most 140 characters from its receipt.
  5. Submit only after selector approval. Save the submission-page receipt and organizer result. Only then label a score ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED.
