1. PhD-Level Forensic Autopsy: Why h33-h33-2-b2 Scored 0.2778 — And How We Surpass It

DTI(P, T) = (TP_w + eps) / (TP_w + 0.2 * FP_w + 0.8 * FN_w + eps), K(u, v) = max(0, 1 - ||u - v||_2 / 3.0 px)
TP_w = sum_{t in T} max_{x in A} P(x) K(x, t), FP_w = sum_{x in A} P(x) [1 - max_{t in T} K(x, t)], FN_w = |T| - TP_w
  • Theorem 1 — Crisp Binary Amplitude Optimality ({0.0, 1.0} vs Soft Probabilities): Differentiating DTI(p * 1_S, T) with respect to constant amplitude p in (0, 1] yields d(DTI)/dp = 0.8 |T| C(S) / [0.8 |T| + 0.2 p (|S| + C(S) - D(S))]^2 > 0 everywhere on (0, 1]. Emitting crisp 1.0 values rather than raw 0.02–0.30 GBDT probabilities lifted early GEMSDOE submissions from ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED: 0.0442 to ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED: 0.1922–0.2778.
  • Theorem 2 — Pointwise-max Submodularity & Collar Exclusion: Because TP_w evaluates the pointwise maximum over a 300 m (3.0 px) radius while FP_w sums additively over every predicted pixel, continuous 1-pixel ridges waste 67% of their false-positive budget on already-covered fault pixels.
